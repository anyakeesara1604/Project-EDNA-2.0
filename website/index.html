02 / THE SETTLEMENT

A city built in orbit.

Seven rotating tori, a central sphere, and advanced docking ports working together to create a permanent home beyond Earth.

Residential Tori (x3)

Homes, recreation, and comfortable living spaces with artificial seasons.

The Central Sphere

The main hub for communication, control, and settlement operations.

Agricultural Tori (x2)

Hydroponic and aeroponic farms that provide food for the settlement.

Industrial Tori (x2)

Spaces for manufacturing, technology, and pharmaceutical production.

Docking Ports

Secure arrival points for spacecraft, cargo, and crew.

03 / LIFE IN ORBIT

Living Beyond Earth.
