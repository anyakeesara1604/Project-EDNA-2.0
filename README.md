# EDNA 2.0 🌍

**An interactive exploration of a futuristic orbital space settlement inspired by the NASA Ames Space Settlement Contest.**

## 🚀 About the Project

EDNA 2.0 is a space settlement concept designed to explore how humans could live in a large-scale orbital habitat.

The project presents a futuristic vision of an orbital settlement, its rotating habitats, and the infrastructure needed to support a community in space.

## 🌌 Project Highlights

- Designed to accommodate approximately **35,000 residents**.
- Consists of **7 rotating toroidal habitats**.
- Includes **2 docking ports**.
- Explores artificial gravity through rotating habitats.
- Estimated project cost: **$971.7 billion**.

### Habitat Specifications

| Parameter | Inner Hybrid Tori | Outer Tori |
|---|---:|---:|
| Radius | 1,000 m | 2,534 m |
| Rotation speed | 0.891 RPM | 0.366 RPM |
| Artificial gravity | 0.89 G | 0.38 G |
